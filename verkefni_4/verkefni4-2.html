SKYLINE GENERATIONS

Nissan Skyline R31

The Nissan Skyline R31 is a classic Japanese sports sedan that was produced from 1985 to 1990. It is part of the long-running Skyline family and is known for its boxy, sharp-edged design, which was popular during the 1980s. The R31 was available with several engines, including the famous RB-series engines. One of the most well-known versions is the Skyline GTS-R, which was developed for Group A touring car racing and featured a turbocharged RB20DET engine. The R31 is especially important because it helped establish the RB engine family that later became famous through cars like the R32 Skyline GT-R. Today, the R31 has a strong following among JDM enthusiasts and collectors who appreciate its unique styling and history.

Nissan Skyline R32

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R32 was introduced in 1989 and quickly became a Japanese performance icon. It was powered by the famous RB26DETT, a 2.6-liter twin-turbocharged inline-six engine known for its strength and tuning potential. The R32 also featured Nissan’s ATTESA E-TS all-wheel-drive system and Super-HICAS four-wheel steering, giving it excellent traction and handling. The R32 became especially famous through motorsport, where it dominated touring-car racing and earned the nickname “Godzilla.” Its combination of advanced technology, lightweight construction, and powerful engine made it extremely competitive. Today, the R32 GT-R is highly respected by car enthusiasts and collectors. It established the foundation for the modern GT-R legend.

Nissan Skyline R33

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R33 was released in 1994 and continued the legacy of the GT-R lineage. It was powered by the RB26DETT engine, maintaining the high-performance standards of its predecessor. The R33 featured improved aerodynamics and a more refined interior compared to the R32. It also included enhancements to the all-wheel-drive system and four-wheel steering, providing better handling characteristics.

Nissan Skyline R34

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 was introduced in 1998 and further refined the GT-R formula. It featured a more powerful RB26DETT engine and continued to be a dominant force in motorsport. The R34 also included improvements to the suspension and aerodynamics, making it even more competitive on the track.

Nissan Skyline R35

The Nissan GT-R R35, introduced in 2007, marked a significant departure from the previous Skyline GT-R models. It was powered by the VR38DETT, a 3.8-liter twin-turbocharged V6 engine, and featured an advanced all-wheel-drive system. The R35 GT-R was designed to compete with high-performance sports cars worldwide and quickly gained a reputation for its incredible acceleration and handling. It has continued to evolve over the years, with various updates and special editions, solidifying its status as a modern performance icon.
