The Mazda Furai is a unique concept car that was introduced by Mazda in 2007. Its name means “sound of the wind” in Japanese, reflecting the car’s focus on speed and aerodynamics. The Furai was designed by Mazda’s North American design team and was inspired by the company’s successful racing cars. Its aggressive shape, low body, and distinctive styling made it stand out from other concept cars.

of the most impressive features of the Mazda Furai was its engine. It used a 3-rotor Wankel rotary engine that produced around 450 horsepower and ran on ethanol-based racing fuel. The Furai was built on a Courage C65 racing chassis, giving it real racing-car performance rather than simply being a design study. Although the Mazda Furai was never put into production, it became one of Mazda’s most memorable concept cars. Its combination of futuristic design, rotary-engine technology, and racing heritage showed Mazda’s creativity and passion for motorsport. The Furai remains an important part of Mazda’s history and continues to be admired by car enthusiasts around the world.
